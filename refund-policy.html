Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

Channel Subscription SaaS provides digital services. Customers should review plan details before making a payment.

Cancellation

A plan may be cancelled for future renewal by contacting support. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund for a period already activated.

Refund eligibility

Refunds may be considered where payment was duplicated, the service was not activated due to a verified technical issue, or where required by applicable law.

Non-refundable situations

  • Change of mind after activation
  • Violation of service rules
  • Telegram account, channel or group restrictions outside our control
  • Failure to use the service during an active plan period

Request process

Contact ehihaha456@gmail.com with payment proof and transaction details. Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment method where possible.
